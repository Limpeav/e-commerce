Design and Development of an E-Commerce Platform for Baby Products with AI-Based Customer Sentiment Analysis at PCHUNCHANACSAMAI CO., LTD.
1. Background of the Study
The rapid growth of information technology and internet usage has significantly transformed the way businesses operate, particularly in the field of e-commerce. Online shopping platforms provide customers with convenience, wider product choices, and easy access to product information and customer reviews. In recent years, e-commerce has become increasingly popular for purchasing essential baby products such as diapers, milk powder, baby clothes, and toys.
For baby product businesses, maintaining product quality and customer trust is crucial, as these products directly affect infant health and safety. Customer feedback and online reviews play an important role in helping parents make purchasing decisions. However, customer reviews are often written in unstructured text form, making it difficult for businesses to analyze feedback efficiently and accurately using manual methods.
Artificial Intelligence (AI), particularly Natural Language Processing (NLP), provides effective solutions for analyzing large volumes of textual data. AI-based customer sentiment analysis can automatically classify customer opinions as positive, negative, or neutral and identify common issues or satisfaction trends. Integrating sentiment analysis into an e-commerce platform enables businesses to gain faster insights into customer behavior and improve product quality, services, and decision-making processes.
Therefore, this study proposes the design and development of an e-commerce platform for baby products integrated with AI-based customer sentiment analysis, using PCHUNCHANACSAMAI CO., LTD. as a case study.

2. Problem Statement
Despite the growth of online baby product sales, many small and medium-sized enterprises still face challenges, including:
Inefficient management of customer reviews and feedback
Difficulty in understanding customer satisfaction and recurring complaints
Lack of data-driven insights to support business decisions
Absence of intelligent features in traditional e-commerce platforms
As a result, businesses struggle to respond effectively to customer needs and improve overall service quality.

3. Objectives of the Study
3.1 General Objective
To design and develop an e-commerce platform for baby products integrated with AI-based customer sentiment analysis.
3.2 Specific Objectives
To design a user-friendly e-commerce system for baby products
To develop core e-commerce functionalities such as product management, ordering, and payment
To implement AI-based sentiment analysis to analyze customer reviews
To classify customer feedback into positive, negative, and neutral sentiments
To provide analytical reports to support business decision-making




4. Scope of the Study
This study focuses on:
Developing a web-based e-commerce platform for baby products
Implementing user roles including administrator and customer
Managing baby product categories, variants, and orders
Analyzing customer reviews using AI sentiment analysis techniques
Conducting the case study at PCHUNCHANACSAMAI CO., LTD.
The study does not include mobile application development or advanced logistics optimization.
5. Significance of the Study
The results of this study are expected to:
Help baby product businesses improve customer satisfaction
Support business decision-making through data-driven insights
Enhance efficiency in managing customer feedback
Provide a practical reference for future research on AI-powered e-commerce systems
Contribute academic knowledge in the fields of e-commerce and artificial intelligence
6. Research Methodology
The research methodology includes:
Requirement Analysis – Studying business requirements and user needs
System Design – Designing system architecture, database, and user interfaces
System Development – Implementing the e-commerce platform and AI components
Testing and Evaluation – Testing system functionality and accuracy of sentiment analysis
Documentation – Writing and compiling the research report
7. Expected Outcomes
A fully functional e-commerce platform for baby products
An AI-based sentiment analysis module for customer reviews
Analytical reports showing customer satisfaction trends
Improved understanding of customer opinions for business improvement
8. Conclusion
This research proposal presents the design and development of an e-commerce platform for baby products integrated with AI-based customer sentiment analysis. The proposed system aims to address existing challenges faced by baby product businesses in managing customer feedback and understanding customer satisfaction through traditional methods.
By integrating artificial intelligence, particularly sentiment analysis using natural language processing, the system is expected to provide valuable insights into customer opinions, enabling businesses to make informed, data-driven decisions. The study focuses on improving product quality, service efficiency, and customer trust, which are critical factors in the baby product industry.
The successful implementation of this research is expected to contribute both practically and academically. Practically, it will support PCHUNCHANACSAMAI CO., LTD. in enhancing its e-commerce operations. Academically, it will serve as a reference for future studies related to e-commerce systems and AI applications. Overall, this proposed research demonstrates the potential of combining e-commerce platforms with artificial intelligence to improve business performance and customer satisfaction.

Requirement

1. User Account & Security Requirements
UR-1.1: As a user, I want to create an account and manage my profile/addresses so that my checkout process is faster.
UR-1.2: As a user, I want to securely reset my password via email if I forget it.
UR-1.3: As an Admin, I want to manage user accounts to ensure platform security and support.
UR-1.4: The system must encrypt sensitive data (passwords, payment tokens) to ensure data protection.
2. Product Catalog & Browsing
UR-2.1: As a customer, I want to search and filter products by price, brand, and type to find specific baby items quickly.
UR-2.2: As an Admin, I want to manage inventory (add, edit, delete, stock levels) to keep the product listing accurate.
UR-2.3: The system must display real-time product availability to prevent customers from ordering out-of-stock items.
3. Shopping Cart & Checkout
UR-3.1: As a customer, I want to manage my shopping cart (add/remove/update) with automatic price calculations including discounts.
UR-3.2: As a customer in Cambodia, I want to pay via KHQR / Bakong or other online methods for a localized payment experience.
UR-3.3: The system must calculate shipping fees automatically based on the selected delivery address.
4. Order & Delivery Management
UR-4.1: As a customer, I want to track my order status from "Confirmed" to "Delivered" in real-time.
UR-4.2: As an Admin, I want a centralized dashboard to update shipping statuses and manage cancellations.
5. AI-Powered Sentiment & Reviews
UR-5.1: As a customer, I want to read and write product reviews to share my experience with other parents.
UR-5.2: As an Admin, I want the system to automatically classify reviews as Positive, Neutral, or Negative using AI.
UR-5.3: As an Admin, I want to view a "Sentiment Summary" and satisfaction insights to identify which products need improvement.
6. Notifications & Support
UR-6.1: As a user, I want to receive automated notifications for order milestones and promotional offers.
UR-6.2: As a customer, I want access to an FAQ and a contact form to resolve issues quickly.
7. Analytics & Reporting
UR-7.1: As an Admin, I want to view sales reports and inventory trends to make data-driven business decisions.
UR-7.2: As an Admin, I want to see feedback trends over time to measure customer loyalty.
